Subsequence is a sequence inside a given input that may be contiguous or non contiguous in nature but
follows the order of input.
ex)
[3,2,1]
Subsequences : [],[3],[1],[2],[3,1],[1,2],[3,2],[3,1,2]========> n sized array have 2^n Subsequences.

    PowerSet.
